Three designs for “Regulator and target along pseudotime”

Real data from your 19-gene project (2e40f8acffeb), lineage PseudoTime1. “On at” is when a gene reaches half of its maximum. One target menu drives all three options.

Option 1 · Upstream tree

Read left to right. Grey arrow: the regulator switches on first. Orange dashed arrow: it switches on later than the gene it regulates. Click a box to follow its paths and see the curves.

Trends of the selected genes

Option 2 · Small multiples

One mini plot per regulator: its curve (blue) against the target (black). No clicking needed. Chains are two mini plots side by side.

Direct regulators

Chains (top 3)

Option 3 · Lag bars

Zero is when the target switches on. A blue bar to the left: the regulator switched on that much earlier. Orange to the right: later. For a chain, the light part is A to B and the dark part is B to the target. Click a row to see the curves.

Trends of the selected genes
