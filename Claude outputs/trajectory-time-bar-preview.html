One time bar per figure, in the same place

Mock-up with made-up expression values and cell counts; only the layout matters. Each block shows the bottom of the cell-trajectory figure and the top-to-bottom of the cascade heatmap, as they sit on the page.

Now

The trajectory figure ends with a colour bar, and the cascade starts with another one a few centimetres below. The cascade also has a separate plain axis at its bottom.

Option A · The colour bar becomes the cascade's axis (my recommendation)

The strip above the heatmap is removed. The plain axis under it is replaced by the same colour bar with ticks that the trajectory figure uses, plus an “early → late” cue. Each figure has exactly one time bar, at its bottom, and each download carries it.

Option B · No colour bar on the cascade on screen, added only in the download

On the page the cascade keeps just its plain numbered axis, so only one colour bar is visible. The downloaded file gets the colour bar added automatically. What you see and what you download differ.
