Option 3 · Arc diagram ordered by switch-on time (recommended)

After Scribe’s arc diagram. The target and its regulators sit evenly spaced on one line, left to right in the order they switch on (the time is printed under each name). An arc over the line means the regulator switched on first; a dashed orange arc under the line means the target did. Arcs into the target are drawn darker.
